Five insights that affect the decision

These patterns support an investigation priority, not a causal claim.

01

Observed churn is 46.7%

Seven of fifteen supplied records are marked churned. Rates are highly sensitive to individual records.

02

Month-to-Month needs scrutiny

All 7 Month-to-Month records are marked churned; the 4 One Year and 4 Two Year records are all not marked churned.

03

Churned records have shorter tenure

Mean tenure is 7.0 months among churned records versus 29.1 months among those not marked churned.

04

Support activity is elevated

Mean tickets in the prior 90 days are 4.3 for churned records versus 1.0 for records not marked churned.

05

Historical charge exposure

Monthly charges sum to ₹409.93 for records already marked churned. This is not a forecast of recoverable revenue.

Recommended decision

Approve a 30-day, human-reviewed support pilot.

First review customers with tenure of 12 months or less and at least 3 tickets in the previous 90 days. Offer help focused on resolving issues, not pressuring an upgrade. Compare later churn and ticket resolution with a pre-defined baseline before scaling.

30-day action plan

WEEK 1

Define and review

Confirm cohort rules, review unresolved ticket themes, and set a baseline and success measures.

WEEKS 2–3

Contact and resolve

Assign an owner, offer a service-first check-in, and log attempted contact, response, and resolution.

WEEK 4+

Measure and decide

Compare subsequent churn, ticket resolution, feedback, and cost. Revise or stop if outcomes do not improve.

Limitations: n = 15 synthetic records; no contact history, ticket topics, satisfaction scores, renewal dates, intervention outcomes, or margin data. The results do not establish causation or validate a predictive model.
