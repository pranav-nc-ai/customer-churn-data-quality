KPI definitions & methodology

Clear definitions make the dashboard auditable and reproducible

METHOD NOTES

Churn rate

Number of records with Churn = Yes divided by all records in the current filtered sample. This is an observation-period rate only insofar as the supplied labels represent that period.

Retention rate

Number of records with Churn = No divided by all records in the current filtered sample. It is the complement of churn rate for these binary labels.

Revenue at risk · monthly proxy

Sum of MonthlyCharges for churned records in the current filter slice. Because these records are already marked churned, this is best interpreted as monthly charges associated with churn, not a forward-looking prediction.

Tenure KPIs & filters

Average tenure is the arithmetic mean of TenureMonths. Tenure cohorts are 0–6, 7–12, 13–24, and 25+ months. Contract, tenure, and payment filters update every metric and visual.

Limitations and responsible use

The dataset has 15 synthetic rows, so a small number of records can materially change each rate. The dashboard describes associations and does not predict churn or prove that contract type or payment method causes churn. Validate any hypothesis on a larger, representative dataset before making customer-contact decisions.
