# Three-Minute Executive Presentation Script

**Approximate duration:** about 3 minutes. The MP4 package uses synthetic narration. Replace it with your own voice recording if the reviewer specifically requires a personal recording.

## Slide 1 — Opening
Today’s decision is where to focus a small retention investigation. The supplied sample has only fifteen synthetic customers, so we will not treat it as a forecast. The recommendation is a short, service-first pilot focused on early-tenure customers showing repeated support activity.

## Slide 2 — Executive summary
Seven of fifteen records are marked churned, an observed rate of 46.7 percent. Monthly charges associated with those records total ₹409.93. That is a historical exposure proxy, not money we can assume is recoverable. Use it for transparent scenario planning only.

## Slide 3 — Five insights
Five facts matter. First, seven of fifteen records churned. Second, all seven Month-to-Month records are marked churned, compared with zero of four One Year and zero of four Two Year records. Third, mean tenure is 7.0 versus 29.1 months. Fourth, average recent support tickets are 4.3 versus 1.0. Fifth, churned records sum to ₹409.93 in monthly charges.

## Slide 4 — Interpreting the pattern
The pattern suggests investigating the early customer experience and recurring support needs. We should ask whether unresolved problems or onboarding friction can be fixed before a renewal decision. These observations do not prove that contract type or tickets cause churn, and the tiny groups make rates unstable.

## Slide 5 — Impact sensitivity
For planning only, applying hypothetical reductions of 10, 25, or 50 percent to the ₹409.93 charge-exposure proxy gives about ₹41, ₹102, or ₹205 per month-equivalent. These are sensitivities, not forecasts or promised savings. They exclude intervention cost, margins, future customer mix, and uncertainty.

## Slide 6 — Thirty-day pilot
Run a 30-day pilot. In week one, review ticket themes and define the cohort: tenure of twelve months or less and at least three tickets in the previous ninety days. In weeks two and three, a customer-success owner offers helpful, human-reviewed check-ins. Then compare subsequent churn and ticket resolution with a pre-defined baseline.

## Slide 7 — Limitations
The evidence is limited: fifteen synthetic records, no contact history, no satisfaction measures, no intervention outcomes, and no margin or outreach cost. The analysis is descriptive; it does not establish causality or validate prediction. Any expansion should use a larger cohort, a defined comparison, and a stated follow-up window.

## Slide 8 — Close
The decision request is to approve the small pilot, assign an owner, and agree on success measures before contact begins. Track resolved issues, customer feedback, and later churn; revise or stop if results do not improve. The goal is measured learning, not overconfident prediction.
