# Evidence Limitations and Metric Definitions

- **Sample:** 15 synthetic customer records.
- **Observed churn rate:** 7 records with Churn=Yes divided by 15 total = 46.7%.
- **Contract rate:** churned records divided by customers in each supplied contract group; denominators are 7, 4, and 4.
- **Tenure and ticket metrics:** descriptive means among churned vs non-churned records; these do not prove causation.
- **Monthly charges associated with churn:** sum of MonthlyCharges for records marked Churn=Yes = ₹409.93. This is a historical exposure proxy, not a forecast.
- **Impact sensitivity:** assumed 10%, 25%, and 50% reductions applied arithmetically to ₹409.93. It is not a measured treatment effect or guaranteed saving.
- **Missing context:** contact history, ticket topic/severity, satisfaction, renewal date, intervention outcomes, revenue margin, outreach cost, and a representative larger sample.
- **Next measurement:** predefine a comparison, follow-up window, and success metrics; compare subsequent churn, ticket resolution, contact success, feedback, and cost.
